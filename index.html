Our Story

For over forty years, our family has cooked the dishes of Chanthaburi, a coastal town in eastern Thailand known for its fresh seafood, fragrant herbs and fruit. We brought those recipes to Taiwan and cook them the way they were cooked at home, with no shortcuts.

Signature Dishes

Pad Thai

Rice noodles, tamarind, palm sugar, egg and crushed peanuts.

Green Curry

Slow-simmered coconut curry with Thai basil and fresh chilli.

Mango Sticky Rice

Sweet glutinous rice, ripe mango and coconut cream.
